I've shaped thousands of beards, and I can tell within seconds of a client sitting down whether he has a routine or just... has a beard. The difference is never genetics. It's four habits, in this order.

## 1. Wash it like you mean it (but not with shampoo)

Your face produces less oil than your scalp, and regular shampoo strips a beard dry. Use a dedicated beard wash two or three times a week — not daily. Overwashing is the number one cause of the itch that makes men shave off a perfectly good beard at week three.

## 2. Oil is non-negotiable

Beard oil isn't a luxury product; it's conditioner for hair that's naturally coarser than what's on your head. Three to six drops, worked into the skin underneath first, then through the beard. Do it after a shower when the pores are open. If your beard feels like wire brush, this is the step you're missing.

## 3. Brush or comb daily

Thirty seconds with a boar-bristle brush trains the hair to grow in the direction you want and distributes oil evenly. It also exfoliates the skin underneath, which prevents the flaky "beardruff" that shows up on dark shirts.

## 4. Trim the edges, respect the lines

Even if you're growing it long, the neckline and cheek lines need maintenance every week or two. The rule for the neckline: two fingers above the Adam's apple, everything below goes. Cheek lines should follow your natural growth — don't carve them too low or the beard looks like a chin strap.
